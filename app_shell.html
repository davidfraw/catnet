Data sources & method

Risk ratings combine the TripSit community-expert combination database (recreational × recreational) with a curated medication layer built from published pharmacology literature and official product information. Every entry carries an evidence tag: well documented (established literature), case reports / consensus, or limited data (theoretical, mechanism-based). Absence of a warning is not proof of safety — many combinations are simply under-studied.

    What CatNet does not cover

    Exact doses and timing, personal factors (weight, tolerance, heart conditions, pregnancy), poly-combinations of 3+ substances beyond pairwise checks, and novel/unknown substances. Nicotine and most vitamins/supplements are omitted (no significant acute interactions). When the situation doesn't fit the tool, trust your training and escalate.
